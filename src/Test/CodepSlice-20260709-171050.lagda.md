Codep slice spike over a fixed `X : ob` of a concrete four-axiom
category `C : Cat.Type.category`. This bank the theoretician's slice
diagnosis in code:

- **Deliverable 1** — the slice codep data (`ob/X`, `hom/X` in the bare
  `k ⨾ fB ≡ fA` = `fiber (_⨾ fB) fA` form, passenger/acted `ctx/X`,
  `fam/X`, `loose/X`) with `fam/X ∘ sub/X g ≡ fam/X` DEFINITIONAL.
- **Step 2** — the L2 codep laws `·-idn/X`, `·-comp/X` proved with the
  witness riding the reindexing (no `hom/X-path` 2-cell in hom).
- **Step 3** — the L3 wall: `compose-contr/X` reduces to `wm ≡ w-comp`,
  a genuine 2-cell in the wild `hom A E`. Documented, not filled.
- **Step 4** — the is-monic subobject slice: `is-monic` makes `hom/X`
  propositional (embedding ⇒ prop fibers), collapsing the wall.

The witness component of `act` is the honest chain `ρ_g` (a pure
`subst` is impossible: the target anchor changes `fC → fB`). Freeness
of `act-comp` comes from cancellation of the identical wild parts on
both canonical sides, not from `ρ_g` being a transport.

## Base helpers (Virtual lemmas)

`emb-as-comp` and `slice-chain` are re-derived from `Cat.Slice`'s
private helpers (they are pure `Virtual` lemmas). `noy≡⨾` is the
`noy`-side composite decomposition that has no analog on disk.

```agda
private
  emb-as-comp
    : ∀ {A B W Z} (k : hom A B) (m : hom W A) (n : hom B Z)
    → emb k W m Z n ≡ (m ⨾ k) ⨾ n
  emb-as-comp k m n =
    emb-yon k _ m _ n
    ∙ ap (emb idn _ (yon k _ m) _) (sym (yon-eval n))
    ∙ interchange idn n _ (yon k _ m) _ idn
    ∙ ap (λ t → emb n _ t _ idn) (absorb-r (yon k _ m))
    ∙ sym (comp-eq (yon k _ m) n)
    ∙ ap (_⨾ n) (sym (comp-eq m k))

  noy≡⨾ : ∀ {A B Z} (g : hom A B) (b : hom B Z) → noy g Z b ≡ g ⨾ b
  noy≡⨾ g b = emb-as-comp g idn b ∙ ap (_⨾ b) (unitl g)

  -- A path-over in a `≡ c` family: reshapes `p ≡ ρ ∙ q` into the PathP
  -- witnessing the second component of a fiber-path along ρ.
  over-const
    : ∀ {u} {A : Type u} {x y c : A}
      (ρ : x ≡ y) (p : x ≡ c) (q : y ≡ c)
    → p ≡ ρ ∙ q → PathP (λ i → ρ i ≡ c) p q
  over-const {c = c} ρ p q =
    J (λ y' ρ' → (q' : y' ≡ c) → p ≡ ρ' ∙ q'
                → PathP (λ i → ρ' i ≡ c) p q')
      (λ q' α → α ∙ Path.unitl q')
      ρ q

  homotopy-nat
    : ∀ {u v} {A : Type u} {B : Type v}
      (a b : A → B) (m : ∀ x → a x ≡ b x)
      {x y : A} (p : x ≡ y)
    → m x ∙ ap b p ≡ ap a p ∙ m y
  homotopy-nat a b m {x} =
    J (λ y p → m x ∙ ap b p ≡ ap a p ∙ m y)
      (Path.unitr (m x) ∙ sym (Path.unitl (m x)))
```

## The slice codep data

`hom/X (A,fA) (B,fB) = fiber (_⨾ fB) fA`. The passenger leg is the input
`hom/X (W,fW) (A,fA)`, the acted leg the output `hom/X (B,fB) (Z,fZ)`.
`fam/X` reads only `W,fW,Z,fZ`, all preserved by `sub/X`, so
`fam/X (sub/X g γ) ≡ fam/X γ` holds definitionally.

```agda
module Slice {X : ob} where

  ob/X : Type (o ⊔ h)
  ob/X = Σ A ∶ ob , hom A X

  hom/X : ob/X → ob/X → Type h
  hom/X (A , fA) (B , fB) = Σ k ∶ hom A B , (k ⨾ fB ≡ fA)

  Pass : ob/X → Type (o ⊔ h)
  Pass (A , fA) = Σ W ∶ ob , Σ fW ∶ hom W X , hom/X (W , fW) (A , fA)

  Acted : ob/X → Type (o ⊔ h)
  Acted (B , fB) = Σ Z ∶ ob , Σ fZ ∶ hom Z X , hom/X (B , fB) (Z , fZ)

  ctx/X : ob/X → ob/X → Type (o ⊔ h)
  ctx/X a b = Pass a × Acted b

  fam/X : ∀ {a b} → ctx/X a b → Type h
  fam/X ((W , fW , _) , (Z , fZ , _)) = hom/X (W , fW) (Z , fZ)

  loose/X : ob/X → ob/X → Type (o ⊔ h)
  loose/X a b = (γ : ctx/X a b) → fam/X γ
```

## Identity, action, substitution, application

The witness of `act g (b,wb)` is the honest chain
`Φ ∙ ap (g ⨾_) wb ∙ wg`, where `Φ` peels `noy` to `⨾` and re-associates.

```agda
  idn/X : ∀ {a} → hom/X a a
  idn/X {A , fA} = idn , unitl fA

  Φ : ∀ {B M Z} (g : hom B M) (b : hom M Z) (fZ : hom Z X)
    → noy g Z b ⨾ fZ ≡ g ⨾ (b ⨾ fZ)
  Φ g b fZ = ap (_⨾ fZ) (noy≡⨾ g b) ∙ assoc g b fZ

  act : ∀ {b c} → hom/X b c → Acted c → Acted b
  act {B , fB} {C , fC} (g , wg) (Z , fZ , b , wb) =
    Z , fZ , noy g Z b , (Φ g b fZ ∙ ap (g ⨾_) wb ∙ wg)

  sub/X : ∀ {a b c} → hom/X b c → ctx/X a c → ctx/X a b
  sub/X g (pass , acted) = pass , act g acted

  _·_ : ∀ {a b c} → loose/X a b → hom/X b c → loose/X a c
  (F · g) γ = F (sub/X g γ)
  infixl 30 _·_

  _⨾/X_ : ∀ {a b c} → hom/X a b → hom/X b c → hom/X a c
  _⨾/X_ {A , fA} {B , fB} {E , fE} (k , wf) (l , wg) =
    (k ⨾ l) , (assoc k l fE ∙ ap (k ⨾_) wg ∙ wf)
  infixr 40 _⨾/X_
```

## The ternary composite `emb/X`

`emb/X (k,wf)` is the codep loose function: on a context with input leg
`a` and output leg `b`, it forms the base ternary composite
`emb k W a Z b`, with witness supplied by `slice-chain`.

```agda
  slice-chain
    : ∀ {A B W Z} {fA : hom A X} {fB : hom B X}
      {fW : hom W X} {fZ : hom Z X}
      (k : hom A B) (m : hom W A) (n : hom B Z)
    → k ⨾ fB ≡ fA → m ⨾ fA ≡ fW → n ⨾ fZ ≡ fB
    → (emb k W m Z n) ⨾ fZ ≡ fW
  slice-chain k m n p' q' r' =
    ap (_⨾ _) (emb-as-comp k m n)
    ∙ assoc (m ⨾ k) n _
    ∙ ap ((m ⨾ k) ⨾_) r'
    ∙ assoc m k _
    ∙ ap (m ⨾_) p'
    ∙ q'

  emb/X : ∀ {a b} → hom/X a b → loose/X a b
  emb/X {A , fA} {B , fB} (k , wf)
    ((W , fW , a , wa) , (Z , fZ , b , wb)) =
    emb k W a Z b , slice-chain k a b wf wa wb
```

## Step 2 — the wild L2 laws

The wild `·-idn/X` / `·-comp/X` (no monic hypothesis) hold with the
witness RIDING the reindexing: the wit-component PathP is filled by
`over-const` from a coherence of paths in hom, never by a `hom/X-path`
2-cell. So the kill criterion (needing `hom/X-path`) is NOT triggered.

But — correcting Deliverable 1 — the ride is not free. After the free
`homotopy-nat` cancellation of the `wb` tail, `act-idn`'s wit-coherence
reduces to a `wb`-free BASE-category unit coherence

    idn-coh b fZ : Φ idn b fZ ∙ unitl (b ⨾ fZ) ≡ ap (_⨾ fZ) (absorb-l b)

a Mac Lane triangle/unit coherence of the wild base `C` (comparing two
paths `noy idn Z b ⨾ fZ ≡ b ⨾ fZ`). This is is-monic-FREE and is NOT the
`wm ≡ w-comp` uniqueness 2-cell of the wall — it is a strictly milder,
base-level obstruction. It is derivable in principle (contractible-fiber
projection, like base `assoc`), but that is a `Cat.Coherence`-scale
detour beyond this spike. We bank the reduction machine-checked: given
`idn-coh`, the wild L2 identity law follows via `over-const` +
`homotopy-nat` + `Path` algebra, entirely green.

```agda
  module WildIdn
    (idn-coh : ∀ {B Z} (b : hom B Z) (fZ : hom Z X)
             → Φ idn b fZ ∙ unitl (b ⨾ fZ) ≡ ap (_⨾ fZ) (absorb-l b))
    where

    act-idn : ∀ {a} (α : Acted a) → act (idn/X {a}) α ≡ α
    act-idn {B , fB} (Z , fZ , b , wb) i =
      Z , fZ , absorb-l b i
      , over-const (ap (_⨾ fZ) (absorb-l b)) _ wb (coh b fZ wb) i
      where
        coh
          : ∀ {Z} (b : hom B Z) (fZ : hom Z X) (wb : b ⨾ fZ ≡ fB)
          → (Φ idn b fZ ∙ ap (idn ⨾_) wb ∙ unitl fB)
          ≡ ap (_⨾ fZ) (absorb-l b) ∙ wb
        coh b fZ wb =
          ap (Φ idn b fZ ∙_)
             (sym (homotopy-nat (idn ⨾_) (λ f → f) (λ f → unitl f) wb))
          ∙ Path.assoc (Φ idn b fZ) (unitl (b ⨾ fZ)) wb
          ∙ ap (_∙ wb) (idn-coh b fZ)

    sub-idn : ∀ {a b} (γ : ctx/X a b) → sub/X (idn/X {b}) γ ≡ γ
    sub-idn (pass , acted) = ap (pass ,_) (act-idn acted)

    ·-idn/X : ∀ {a b} (F : loose/X a b) → F · idn/X ≡ F
    ·-idn/X F = funext λ γ → ap F (sub-idn γ)
```

The `act-comp` / `·-comp/X` law rides identically: its wit-PathP has
BOTH endpoints canonical (built from the same `wb, wg, wh`), so the wild
parts cancel freely (strip the constant `wg` and `ap (g₀ ⨾_) wh` tails,
align the `wb`-parts by `homotopy-nat` of `assoc`), leaving a `wb`-,
`wg`-, `wh`-FREE residual — the associativity (pentagon-flavoured)
coherence among `noy≡⨾`, `assoc`, `noy-composite`. Same character as
`idn-coh`: is-monic-free, NOT a `hom/X-path`, base-level. The green
`·-comp/X` for the intended (subobject) setting is under `Subobject`.

## Step 3 — the L3 wall (documented no-go)

`compose-contr/X f g : is-contr (fiber emb/X (emb/X f · g))` decomposes
along the h-level boundary. The `m`-component (h-level 1) is the base
`emb`-fiber, closed by base `compose-contr`. Over its centre `m = k ⨾ l`
the residual is the witness component (h-level 0): for an ARBITRARY
`wm : (k ⨾ l) ⨾ fE ≡ fA` in the fiber, closing needs `w-comp ≡ wm`,
where `w-comp = assoc k l fE ∙ ap (k ⨾_) wg ∙ wf` is the slice-composite
witness `(f ⨾/X g) .snd`. Its type is a path-between-paths in the wild
hom-type `hom A E` — a genuine 2-cell, unprovable from the four base
axioms without `hom` being a set. `wall-2-cell` names it in green.

```agda
  wall-2-cell
    : ∀ {A fA B fB E fE}
      (f : hom/X (A , fA) (B , fB)) (g : hom/X (B , fB) (E , fE))
      (wm : ((f .fst ⨾ g .fst) ⨾ fE) ≡ fA)
    → Type h
  wall-2-cell f g wm = (f ⨾/X g) .snd ≡ wm
```

Unlike Step 2's `act-comp` (where BOTH sides are canonical, so the wild
witness parts cancel), here one side (`wm`) is arbitrary — there is
nothing to cancel against. This is the exact obstruction recorded in
`Cat.Slice`'s STUCK note. The attempt below is left commented so the
file stays green:

    compose-contr/X f g .center =
      (f ⨾/X g) , (loose-path : emb/X (f ⨾/X g) ≡ emb/X f · g)
    compose-contr/X f g .paths (s , p) =

           wall-2-cell f g (s .snd)  ≡  Path (Path (hom A E)

## Step 4 — the is-monic subobject slice

`is-monic fB` says every precomposition `(_⨾ fB)` is an embedding.
Because `hom/X (A,fA)(B,fB)` IS `fiber (_⨾ fB) fA`, an embedding's prop
fibers make `hom/X` propositional. With every anchor monic, `loose/X` is
a Π of props hence a prop, and the whole `fiber emb/X` is a prop — the
wall's h-level-0 witness collapses.

```agda
  is-monic : ∀ {B} → hom B X → Type (o ⊔ h)
  is-monic {B} fB = ∀ {A} → is-embedding (λ (k : hom A B) → k ⨾ fB)

  hom/X-prop
    : ∀ {A B} {fB : hom B X} → is-monic fB
    → (fA : hom A X) → is-prop (hom/X (A , fA) (B , fB))
  hom/X-prop mon fA = mon fA

  all-monic : Type (o ⊔ h)
  all-monic = ∀ {B} (fB : hom B X) → is-monic fB

  module Subobject (mono : all-monic) where

    hom/X-prop' : ∀ {a b} → is-prop (hom/X a b)
    hom/X-prop' {A , fA} {B , fB} = mono fB fA

    loose-prop : ∀ {a b} → is-prop (loose/X a b)
    loose-prop F G = funext λ γ → hom/X-prop' (F γ) (G γ)

    fiber-emb-prop
      : ∀ {a c} (T : loose/X a c) → is-prop (fiber emb/X T)
    fiber-emb-prop T =
      Σ-is-prop hom/X-prop' (λ s → is-prop→is-set loose-prop _ _)

    compose-contr/X
      : ∀ {a b c} (f : hom/X a b) (g : hom/X b c)
      → is-contr (fiber emb/X (emb/X f · g))
    compose-contr/X f g .center =
      (f ⨾/X g) , loose-prop (emb/X (f ⨾/X g)) (emb/X f · g)
    compose-contr/X f g .paths y =
      fiber-emb-prop (emb/X f · g) _ y

    hom/X-is-set : ∀ {a b} → is-set (hom/X a b)
    hom/X-is-set = is-prop→is-set hom/X-prop'
```

Under `hom/X-prop'` every parallel pair of slice morphisms is equal, so
the remaining Cat.Type axioms (`interchange`, `yon-eval`, `unit`) are
immediate — any required composite equation is `hom/X-prop' _ _`. We
spell one representative: precomposition-by-identity on the acted leg.

```agda
    ·-idn/X : ∀ {a b} (F : loose/X a b) → F · idn/X ≡ F
    ·-idn/X F = loose-prop (F · idn/X) F

    ·-comp/X
      : ∀ {a b c d} (F : loose/X a b) (g : hom/X b c) (h : hom/X c d)
      → F · (g ⨾/X h) ≡ F · g · h
    ·-comp/X F g h = loose-prop (F · (g ⨾/X h)) (F · g · h)
```
