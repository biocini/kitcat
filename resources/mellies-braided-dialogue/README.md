## Summaries

Everything below records what the source states; every mathematical
claim is CONJECTURED until machine-checked.

**Abstract.** A dialogue category is a monoidal category equipped
with an exponentiating object ⊥ (its *tensorial pole*): every
object x acquires a left negation x ⊸ ⊥ and a right negation
⊥ ⟜ x, and x is *not* required to coincide with its double
negation. The paper formulates two non-commutative notions of
dialogue category — *helical* and *balanced* — and shows that the
category Mod(H) of left H-modules **of arbitrary dimension** over a
ribbon Hopf algebra H is a balanced dialogue category whose
tensorial pole is the underlying field k, recovering the well-known
ribbon structure on the full subcategory of finite-dimensional
modules.

**Section map.** §1 Introduction; §2 A fractional notation; §3
Helical dialogue categories; §4 Intermezzo: the dialogical twist;
§5 Ribbon categories; §6 Balanced dialogue categories; §7 Four
illustrations.

**Key definitions** (numbering per the manuscript):

- Definition 1 (∗-autonomous category): a dialogue category whose
  tensorial pole is dualizing (the two canonical morphisms
  x → (⊥ ⟜ x) ⊸ ⊥ and x → ⊥ ⟜ (x ⊸ ⊥) are isomorphisms).
- Definitions 2–3 (helical presheaf; helical dialogue category);
  Definition 4 (left name ⌜f⌝ of a morphism).
- Definitions 5–7 (cyclic dialogue categories; cyclic ∗-autonomous
  categories).
- Definitions 8–11 (braiding; balanced category; dual pairs; ribbon
  category) — the classical notions recalled.
- Definition 12 (balanced dialogue category) and Definition 13
  (ultra-thin pole).
- Definitions 15–20 (§7): bialgebra, braided bialgebra, thin Hopf
  algebra, ribbon Hopf algebra — the algebraic inputs to the Mod(H)
  theorem.
- Definition 21: helical/cyclic ∗-autonomous categories, closing
  the loop with the involutive case.

**Framing.** The introduction positions the work between quantum
groups/low-dimensional topology (cyclic categories of Freyd–Yetter,
ribbon categories of Turaev, Shum, Joyal–Street) and the
tensorial-logic program: the duality A ↦ A* is not assumed
involutive, and the dialogue-categorical account is designed to
recover the topological notions at the involutive boundary.
