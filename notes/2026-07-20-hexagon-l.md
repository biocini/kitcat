## The settlement

**H2 does not derive from H1.** The two hexagons are independent
data, classically and here. On a skeletal strict model (π₀ a
commutative monoid, π₁ the loop family, braiding a function
`c : π₀ × π₀ → π₁`), H1 says `c` is multiplicative in its right
slot, H2 in its left; `c(m,n) = f(m)·n` with `f` non-additive
satisfies H1 and refutes H2. The formulation adds no symmetry
that would tie the slots: the flank swap `s U V : A ▵₀ B ≡
B ▿₀ A` is never identified with the reverse route through
`s V U` — that identification is the *symmetric* axiom, and
"H2 from H1 by symmetry" is the symmetric layer's theorem
(classically: a symmetric pre-braiding satisfying one hexagon
satisfies the other). It has no home in `braided-coherent`.

**The field is the full-braid mirror of H1, not the swap-half.**
The braided field-shape ruling anticipated the ι-half and
swap-half getting their own coherence layers at H2 — the ι-half
discharged through `⊗₀-interchange-natural` and `ι-mult-l₀`, the
field stating only the swap's multiplicativity. Ruled out on
statement shape: the swap-half's subject `s (U ●₀ V) W` runs
`(F ▿₀ G) ▵₀ H ≡ H ▿₀ F ▿₀ G`, and `(F ▿₀ G) ▵₀ H` has *no*
strict redistribution into single-factor flanks — `▿₀` and `▵₀`
are each strictly associative and mix across `(F ▵₀ G) ▿₀ H ≐
F ▵₀ (G ▿₀ H)`, but a `▿₀`-block in a `▵₀`-left-slot only moves
along interchange lines. Solving H2 for the swap-half leaves a
six-conjugator composite of whiskered `ι`s — the same content
wearing conjugators, not a smaller datum. The braid statement is
composable and parallel bare, by the same ternary strictness as
H1, and the record stays one shape: two mirror fields per grade.

**Consequence: the anticipated ι-mult/naturality consumer
dissolves.** With the full-braid field, neither the H2 field nor
its derived theory touches `⊗₀/⊗₁-interchange-natural` or the
`ι-mult` hypotheses — the derivation mirrors H1's leaf-for-leaf
(assoc-σ, braid-σ, one `μ`, one `ρ`). Their consumer is the
presentation-equivalence theorem between the braid field and the
conjugated swap-half decomposition — comparison material for
`Cat.Monoidal.Properties` with zero spine consumers, per the
instance-shape discipline, deferred until wanted.
